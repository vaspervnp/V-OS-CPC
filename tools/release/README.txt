V-OS {VERSION}
==========

A graphical operating system for the Amstrad CPC 6128, written in Z80
assembly: windows, menus, an AMX mouse, six languages, several programs
at once, and its own programs (NOTEPAD, PAINT, SHEET, DATABASE, DIARY,
MAIL, FTP, TELNET, MUSIC, Studio and games).

    discs/      vos.dsk (drive A:) and apps.dsk (drive B:), and the
                programs that come on a disc of their own
    manual/     the manual: V-OS.pdf in Greek, V-OS-en.pdf in English,
                and in French, Spanish, German and Italian
    pictures/   V-OS on the screen
    videos/     a tour of V-OS in each language, with sound

Starting: vos.dsk in A:, apps.dsk in B:, then in BASIC

    RUN"VOS

A real 3" disc holds 180K, so V-OS is on two discs. A 128K CPC 6128 is
enough; a memory expansion (up to 576K) runs more programs at once.

On a real CPC: chapter 9 of the manual has a list of what to try and
how to tell us what happened. V-OS has been tested in emulators only,
so each report helps.

The source, and where to report: https://github.com/vaspervnp/V-OS-CPC

WELCOME TO V-OS

V-OS turns the Amstrad CPC 6128 into a
desktop computer: windows, menus, a
mouse, and its own programs.

- F1 the Desktop, F2 the Launcher,
  F3 the Commander, F4 Setup
- drives A:, B: and the M4 SD card
- several programs at once
- NOTEPAD, PAINT, SHEET, DATABASE,
  MUSIC, MARKDOWN, games...
- Studio: programs made of blocks
- six languages, one character set

Written in Z80 assembly. RUN"VOS

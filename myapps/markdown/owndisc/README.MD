# MARKDOWN for V-OS

A **Markdown** editor for the *Amstrad CPC 6128*.
Press ^P to see this page, and ^P again to edit it.

## Text styles

- **bold** with two stars or __underscores__
- *italic* with one star or _underscore_
- ***both*** at once
- `code` between backticks
- a [link](https://example.com) is underlined
- snake_case_names stay as they are, and 2 * 3 = 6

## Lists and quotes

1. Headings: one to three # marks.
2. Lists: -, * or + and a space.
3. Long lines wrap at a space, under the start of their text, so a long item like this one stays tidy on the page.

> Quotes have a bar and are in italic.
